## KaamSetu pilot agreement

Between **[business name]**, owned by **[owner name]**, mobile **[number]** ("the business"),
and **[Sadik's full name]**, maker of KaamSetu ("we").

Start date: **[date]**. Pilot length: **[8 to 10] weeks**, until **[date]**, or longer if both agree in writing (WhatsApp is fine).

### 1. What the pilot is

KaamSetu is new. The business uses it for its real jobs, customers and bills, and tells us what works and what does not. We fix problems and improve the app during the pilot.

### 2. Cost

The pilot is **free**. Nothing is charged for the pilot period, and the business is not asked for payment details.

What happens after the pilot (price, discount for being the first business, or stopping) is decided together before the pilot ends and written down. **[Q-13: fill in once agreed, e.g. "first 6 months after the pilot at ₹… per month".]** If nothing is agreed, the business can keep using the app free for 30 days after the pilot while we decide.

### 3. The business's data stays the business's

- Customers, ACs, jobs, reports and bills that the business enters **belong to the business**.
- We use them only to run the app for the business and to fix problems. We do not sell them, show them to other businesses, or use them for advertising.
- The owner can download everything at any time: **More › Download business data** in the app.
- If the business stops, the owner can close the business in the app (**More › Delete my account**). All its data is permanently deleted 30 days later. Backups are deleted within a further 14 days.
- Data is stored in India (Mumbai). The services we use are listed in the privacy policy.

### 4. The customers' personal data

The business's customers' names, mobile numbers and addresses are personal data. The business collects them and decides what to enter; we store them safely for the business. The business:
- enters only what it needs to do the work,
- tells customers, if asked, that it keeps their details in a job app,
- removes staff who leave (**More › Employees › Remove from business**) the same day.

We:
- keep the data protected (encrypted connections, each business sees only its own data, staff see only their team's jobs),
- tell the owner the same day if we find that data was exposed, and what we are doing about it.

### 5. What we ask of the business

- Use KaamSetu for the pilot team's jobs, and from week **[3]** for all teams.
- Tell us problems quickly on WhatsApp **[support number]**, with a screenshot if possible.
- A 30-minute weekly check-in with us.
- During the first weeks, keep the usual notebook or WhatsApp record **as a backup**, until both agree it is not needed.

### 6. What we promise, and what we cannot

- We answer WhatsApp messages on working days within **[4 hours]**, and fix problems that stop work as fast as we can.
- We back up the data every night.
- Because the app is new, it may have mistakes and may sometimes be unavailable. Work saved on a phone without internet is sent when the phone is online again. We cannot guarantee that nothing ever goes wrong, and we are not responsible for business lost because the app did not work, beyond fixing the problem and helping recover the data.
- Bills made in KaamSetu are the business's bills. The owner checks amounts and GST before sending them. **[Until the accountant confirms the GST bill format (Q-04), say so here.]**

### 7. Stopping

Either side can stop the pilot at any time with a WhatsApp message. If the business stops, we help the owner download their data first.

### 8. Feedback and the name

We may use what we learn (not the business's data) to improve KaamSetu. We mention the business by name, for example as a reference for other businesses, **only with the owner's written permission**.
