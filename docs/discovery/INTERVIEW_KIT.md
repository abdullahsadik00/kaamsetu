# KaamSetu — Phase 1 Discovery Kit

Everything you need for tasks T1.1–T1.5. Take this on your phone. Write answers into [DISCOVERY_FINDINGS.md](DISCOVERY_FINDINGS.md) the same day, while you remember the details.

## Golden rules

1. **Ask about the past, not the future.** "What happened with the last enquiry?" beats "Would you use a feature that…?" People are bad at predicting what they'd use.
2. **Ask for real examples.** "Show me" is the most useful phrase. Look at the WhatsApp chat, the notebook, the bill.
3. **Don't show the prototype until the end** (or the next visit). Otherwise you hear about your design, not their work.
4. **Don't sell or defend.** If they say something we got wrong, write it down and say "thank you, that's useful".
5. **Write their exact words.** The words they use become the app's labels (e.g. "bill" not "invoice").
6. **Protect personal data.** Photograph documents only with customer names and phone numbers covered.

## Part A — Owner interview (T1.1, 60–90 min)

Questions marked **★** answer an open decision (Q-xx in `DECISION_LOG.md`); don't leave without them.

### A1. Warm-up (5 min)
1. How did you start the business? How many teams and people today?
2. What does a normal day look like for you, from morning to night?

### A2. Enquiries (10 min)
3. Show me the last 3 enquiries on WhatsApp. What happened with each one?
4. How many enquiries in a normal day? In peak season (April–June)?
5. Where do you note an enquiry today: memory, notebook, WhatsApp star, Excel?
6. In the last month, did any customer get missed or forgotten? What happened?
7. Do some customers ask for a price first and book later? How do you follow up? **(enquiry vs job, D-007)**
8. ★ **Q-05:** Do you send a quotation/estimate before the work? Written or verbal? How often?

### A3. Scheduling and assignment (10 min)
9. ★ **Q-06:** When you give a customer a time, what do you say exactly: "morning", "after 2", "11 o'clock"? Who confirms the final time with the customer, you or the technician?
10. How do you decide which team gets a job: area, skill, who is free?
11. How do you tell the team about the job today? (WhatsApp message, call, in person?) Show me one.
12. How often does a helper move to another team for a day? Who decides?
13. Are there jobs that need two teams, or more than one day?

### A4. During and after the job (10 min)
14. How do you know a job has started, is stuck, or is finished?
15. When a part is needed, what happens? Who buys it, who pays, how is it added to the bill?
16. What are the common reasons a job needs another visit?
17. After the job, what does the technician tell you? What do you write down?
18. Do you keep any record of each customer's ACs (brand, ton, last service)?

### A5. Money (15 min) — most important section
19. ★ **Q-03:** Who collects money at the site? Cash, UPI to whose number, or bank transfer later?
20. ★ **Q-03:** If the technician collects cash, when and how is it handed over? Has there ever been a mismatch?
21. How do you make a bill today? Show me one (cover the customer details).
22. ★ **Q-04:** Do you give GST bills? To which customers (companies, societies, homes)? Who prepares them?
23. Which regular customers pay later (offices, shops)? How do you track what they owe?
24. How do you decide prices? Is there a fixed price list for servicing, gas filling, installation?

### A6. People and phones (5 min)
25. ★ **Q-01:** Do helpers have smartphones? Should they see anything in the app, or is it enough that the senior technician sees the job?
26. ★ **Q-11:** Which phones do your technicians use? Roughly how old?
27. Which language does each person prefer to read: English, Hindi, Marathi?
28. ★ **Q-07:** Are you ever without internet when an enquiry comes in (travelling, at a site)?

### A7. Closing (10 min)
29. If the app could fix only **one** problem for you, which one?
30. What would make you stop using the app after a week?
31. What would make you stop using the notebook/WhatsApp for this?
32. *Now* show the prototype home screen and the "New job" flow. Watch, don't explain. Note where they hesitate.
33. Who should I talk to next? Can I go with a team on [day]?

## Part B — Senior technician ride-along (T1.2, 2–3 h)

Observe first, ask second. Note the **time** of each step.

**Watch for:**
- [ ] How the job details arrive (message, call, owner in person).
- [ ] Whether the customer is called before leaving, and what is said.
- [ ] How the address is found (Google Maps link, landmarks, calls).
- [ ] Mobile network at the site: basement? lift? Note where it drops. **(offline, Q-10)**
- [ ] Tools/parts carried; what was forgotten or had to be bought.
- [ ] Who does what between senior and helper.
- [ ] What the customer is told at the end; whether money changes hands, how much, which method. **(Q-03)**
- [ ] What the owner is told after the job, and how (call, voice note, WhatsApp text, photo).

**Ask (in the vehicle or after the job):**
1. How do you get your jobs for the day? What information is often missing?
2. What is the most annoying part of a job that isn't the actual AC work?
3. Do you write anything down today? Where?
4. Do you take photos of the work? Why or why not?
5. ★ **Q-10:** Do you have enough mobile data every month? Would uploading 3–4 photos per job be a problem?
6. ★ **Q-11:** Which phone do you use? (Check Settings → About phone: model, Android version, free storage.)
7. Which language do you read most comfortably?
8. If the owner asked you to update a phone app after every job, what would stop you?
9. *At the end:* hand over the prototype in the technician role. Say: "Today's job is in here. Show me how you'd finish it." Watch, don't help. Note every hesitation.

## Part C — Helper conversation (T1.3, 15–20 min)

Keep it relaxed; the helper may be shy with the owner nearby.
1. What do you do on a normal job?
2. Do you have your own smartphone? WhatsApp?
3. ★ **Q-01:** Would it help you to see the job details yourself (address, what to carry), or do you just go with your senior?
4. Have you ever gone to a site alone, before your senior?
5. Which language do you prefer to read?

## Part E — Accountant (T1.5, 10 min call or WhatsApp)

1. Is the business GST-registered (regular or composition scheme)?
2. Which services are billed with GST, and at which rate (servicing, repair, parts, installation, AMC)?
3. Which SAC/HSN codes do you use?
4. Intra-state only (CGST + SGST), or also IGST for customers outside Maharashtra?
5. Any rules for invoice numbering and mandatory fields on the bill?
6. Do you need anything exported from the app every month (sales register)?
